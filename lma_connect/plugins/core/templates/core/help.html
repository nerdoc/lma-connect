{# ─── Abstracts ─── #}

{% trans "Abstracts & posters" %}

{% blocktrans %}Browse all submitted abstracts in the Abstracts tab. Use the search box to filter by title or author, and the pills to narrow down by category. Each abstract page tells you where to find the poster (poster ID + location) and lists all authors with their affiliations and ORCID.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Audience Choice Award: every attendee has three stars for their favourite posters. You give a star by scanning the QR code on the poster itself — voting works on site only, one star per poster. You are asked to confirm, because a star cannot be changed or taken back afterwards. Giving a star also earns you points. Your remaining stars are shown on your profile page.{% endblocktrans %}

{# ─── Abstract results ─── #}

{% trans "Abstract review results" %}

{% blocktrans %}The Abstracts area in Operations aggregates the reviewers' scores per abstract, so the scientific committee can rank submissions and decide on oral vs. poster slots.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Audience vote is the separate public ranking: how many stars each poster collected from attendees. From there you award the Audience Choice Award with one click — a tie at the top is flagged, and only one abstract per event can hold the award. An abstract is only votable once it is marked as poster on display, which also decides whether it gets a QR sheet.{% endblocktrans %}
